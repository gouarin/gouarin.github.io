- Samurai is a new generation of data structures for mesh adaptation methods.
- The library is written in C++ and relies heavily on xtensor.
- It lets users benefit from the latest advances in mesh adaptation transparently in their numerical simulations.
- Samurai is used and developed by PhD students and postdocs of the laboratory.
- Several external collaborations are emerging (ONERA, École centrale, ...).
