# Homepage surface brief

Scope: one bilingual page (`/fr/`, `/en/`) plus the root language redirect. Visitor mode: Read, with a first viewport that holds attention.

Audience and job: academic visitors from a paper, talk or search. They need who he is, then his software, his training courses, and his CV and publications.

Pinned by the owner (2026-09-28 redesign): intro with avatar `loic_small.png`, then cards with a simple pencil drawing per software, then training, then links to CV and publications. Discreet navigation. Clean, attention-holding, beautiful reading type. The card-catalog world was rejected.

Unresolved (never invent): email to publish, training list, CV link, publications link (HAL/ORCID/Scholar).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A researcher drawn in his own line: the ink-and-cyan portrait sets one visual language, and every project is sketched in that same hand. Refuses the photo-plus-news academic template and every skeuomorphic office material.

OWN-WORLD: White page, black ink, one cyan (#67c8dd) used only as spot color and pencil hatching, light grey (#f3f3f3) discs and panels echoing the avatar. Bricolage Grotesque for display, Literata for reading. Graphite pencil line drawings with cyan hatching.

STORY: Visitor meets the portrait and the name, reads one sentence of who he is, scrolls to three drawn software cards, then training, then CV and publications.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Quiet one-line nav at top. Avatar large on the left, name in large display type beside it, a two-sentence introduction in Literata at lead size, contact links underneath. The first software drawings begin at the fold.

FORM: Brief-pinned by the owner, anchored on the incumbent avatar; no concept roll (the owner specified structure and aesthetic after rejecting roll 325095d7). Signature interaction: each pencil drawing draws itself when it enters the viewport; static under reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
